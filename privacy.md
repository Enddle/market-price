# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026

**Extension Name:** Market Price

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines how **Market Price** handles your data when you use this Chrome extension.

## 2. Data Collection and Usage

* **Local Storage Only:** All data, settings, or user preferences are stored exclusively on your local computer. There is no external servers, nor data is ever transmitted, uploaded, or shared off your device.

* **Temporary Current Tab Processing:** The extension only accesses data from your currently active tab to perform its primary functionality. This information is processed in-memory and saved only temporarily while the tab is active or as needed for immediate tool operations.

* **No Browsing History Tracked:** We do not track, monitor, collect, or store your browsing history, visited URLs, search queries, or web activity.

## 3. Third-Party Services and APIs

* **Third-Party API Usage:** This extension utilizes a third-party API (Finnhub, https://finnhub.io/) to process requests and deliver core functionalities.

* **API Usage Data:** When requests are sent to the third-party service, the API provider may automatically generate or log standard network and usage data (such as request timestamps or IP addresses). Our extension does not collect, store, or operate this usage data.

* **Third-Party Policies:** We encourage you to review the third-party provider's privacy policy to understand how they manage API traffic and data.

* **No Data Sales or Transfers:** Beyond the direct communication required to process your requests via the third-party API, your information is never sold, rented, or shared with external parties or analytics providers.
